Theme Song Volume

Set your global theme volume in Account settings → Display, beside Jellyfin's theme-song option. Reset is available there after checking its confirmation box.

On a movie or series page, expand Theme volume. Move the slider to create an override for that title's theme songs. Check Use global to remove the override and return the slider to your global volume.

Settings belong to your account and are shared between compatible clients. A song's override replaces your default. The regular playback volume still acts as the master volume.

Requirements

Jellyfin 12.3 and File Transformation 3.x. Enable theme songs in your Jellyfin display settings.

The web interface must be served by this Jellyfin server. Native or separately hosted clients that do not load the integration are unaffected.

Removal

Uninstall this plugin through Jellyfin, restart the server, and reload open clients. Its saved volume settings are removed during uninstall. Original audio files are never changed.
